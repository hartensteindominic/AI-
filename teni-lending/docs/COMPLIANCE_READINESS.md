# Compliance Readiness

Software implementation is not legal authorization to lend. Before enabling production in any U.S. jurisdiction, obtain qualified review of licensing or partner structure, small-dollar lending rules, rate/fee/APR limits, disclosures, TILA where applicable, ECOA/fair-lending, adverse-action rules, privacy, e-sign consent, payment authorization, servicing, collections, complaints, KYC/AML/sanctions where applicable, data security and record retention. Production remains feature-flagged off until approved.
